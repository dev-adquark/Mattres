Methodology · Scoring model v1.4

How the Match Score works

A transparent, rules-based model. Every recommendation ships with the inputs and thresholds that produced it — nothing is a black box.

The model

Six scoring categories

Your sleep profile is weighed against each category below. Weights shown are defaults — some shift slightly based on your sleep position and weight range.

20% weight

Pressure relief

How well the surface cushions shoulders and hips relative to your body weight and sleep position.

25% weight

Support & alignment

Whether the mattress keeps your spine neutral given your weight band and preferred firmness.

15% weight

Heat & airflow

Airflow and heat-trapping tendencies of the materials, weighted higher if you told us you sleep warm.

15% weight

Motion isolation

How much movement transfers across the surface — weighted higher for couples and light sleepers.

15% weight

Edge support

Stability and sag risk when sitting or sleeping near the perimeter of the mattress.

10% weight

Responsiveness

How quickly the surface adapts as you change position through the night.

From inputs to score

Four steps, no guesswork

1

Sleep profile

Position, weight range, firmness preference, temperature and motion needs.

2

Rule matching

Your inputs are matched against thresholds defined per mattress type and body-weight band.

3

Category scoring

Each of the six categories is scored 0–10 using normalized spec and review data.

4

Weighted total

Category scores are combined using the weights above into a single 0–100 Match Score.

Guardrails

Risk flags

Flags surface mismatches a plain star rating would hide — each one names the trigger and a mitigation.

FlagTypical triggerMitigation
Support threshold mismatch Firmness falls outside the comfort band for your weight and position. Consider a firmer or softer variant in the same line, or add a topper.
Heat retention likelihood All-foam core with limited airflow, flagged for warm sleepers. Look for gel-infused foam, hybrid coils, or a cooling cover.
Edge support concern Reinforced perimeter absent or rated low by verified reviewers. Fine for centered sleepers; worth weighing if you sit on the edge often.
Durability / sag risk Low-density foam layers paired with above-average body weight. Check warranty sag thresholds and trial period before buying.
Data quality

Data sources & confidence

Specs and review themes are normalized into structured tags (e.g. “sleeps hot”, “great edge support”) and each mattress carries a confidence rating based on how much verified data backs it.

Affiliate & sponsored listing disclosures

How we make money, and how we keep it separate from what we recommend.

How we make money

Mattress Match Score is free to use. We earn a commission when you buy through certain links on this site, and some listings are paid placements from mattress brands and retailers. Neither arrangement is required for a mattress to appear in your results.

Affiliate links

“View at retailer” buttons route through an affiliate link. If you make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate links are tagged with tracking parameters so the retailer can credit the referral — they never change the price you pay.

This site contains affiliate links. As an affiliate, we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases, in accordance with FTC guidelines on endorsements and disclosures.

Sponsored listings

Brands and retailers can pay to appear in a labeled “Sponsored” slot. Sponsored listings are refreshed on a set schedule — the badge shows the date a listing's specs and pricing were last verified.

Sponsored slots are visually and structurally separate from algorithmic rankings — a sponsored listing is never blended into, or substituted for, a mattress's Match Score position.

Editorial independence

Match Scores are generated by the scoring model described in our methodology. Advertisers cannot purchase a higher score, alter sub-scores, or remove a risk flag.

Questions about a listing?

If something looks off — outdated pricing, a missing disclosure, a flag you disagree with — tell us and we'll look into it.
