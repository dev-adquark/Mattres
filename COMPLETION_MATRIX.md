# Completion matrix

Mattress Match Score rebuild: audited requirement by requirement against the master briefs (v1-v4 functional and visual, v5 React/TypeScript migration, v6 RTINGS pipeline). Dated 2026-10-08.

**149 requirements: 141 PASS, 6 PARTIAL, 0 FAIL, 2 BLOCKED.** PARTIAL counts as not done and is listed below.

## How this was verified

- Rows were audited against a running production build at 1920, 1440, 1024, 820 and 390 widths (plus reduced motion), then re-checked after four certification rounds and the final clean-tree gate.
- Final gate (clean `.next`): lint 0 problems · typecheck 0 errors · 845 unit tests · production build (120 static pages) · 86 end-to-end tests (desktop, mobile, reduced-motion) · repo-root suite · `npm audit --omit=dev` 0 vulnerabilities.
- Lighthouse (production build), Accessibility / Best Practices / SEO = **100** on home, quiz, catalog, product, compare, guide and methodology at mobile and desktop. Performance: desktop 99-100; mobile 98-100 with real throttling, 84-94 under Lighthouse's pessimistic simulated slow-4G model.
- Final screenshots (1920/1440/1024/820/390, quiz, reveal, results, catalog, product with photo and with render, X-ray, compare, guide, methodology, mobile menu) were reviewed by eye.

## Known limitations

1. **Imagery.** No licensed product photography exists. 14 of 37 mattresses show RTINGS' own photo, credited "Photo: RTINGS" and linked to the review (we do not own it; copyright risk accepted by the owner, switchable in `lib/rtings/photo.ts`). The other 23 show labeled 3D renders, which look computer-generated.
2. **Simulated mobile LCP.** Lighthouse's simulated slow-4G run reports 2.9-4.4 s (budget 2.5 s). Real-throttled mobile LCP is 1.5-2.0 s. Cause: about 293 KB of first-load JavaScript across 21 scripts.
3. **RTINGS content.** The actor returned test scores, dates and photos but no verdicts, pros, cons or overall scores, so none are displayed.
4. **Affiliate links.** No affiliate program or retailer links exist; CTAs are "Visit {Brand}" or an honest unavailable state. The affiliate path (`Check Price at {Retailer}` and `affiliate_click`) is built and unit-tested with fixtures only.
5. **Data gaps shown honestly.** Of 37 mattresses: 14 verified, 21 partially verified, 2 unknown; Queen price missing for 13, warranty for 16, cooling rating for 19. These read "Not yet verified" or "Data unavailable".
6. **Production rollout.** Migration 0005 is not yet applied to the production database and this branch is not deployed or pushed (see the BLOCKED RTINGS row). The Apify token was pasted in chat and should be rotated.
7. **Reference comparison.** The Dribbble reference could not be opened and compared directly.
